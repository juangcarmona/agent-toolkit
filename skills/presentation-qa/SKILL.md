# Presentation QA

Use after building or changing a deck.

Check:

- the narrative still answers the stated audience need;
- titles name the subject or supported takeaway;
- evidence and assumptions are distinguishable;
- no text is clipped, overlapped, microscopic, or stranded;
- visuals carry meaning and the deck does not repeat one layout mechanically;
- Slidev or PowerPoint assets remain editable where required.

Inspect the rendered artifact, not only the source. Record concrete findings and fix material defects before calling the deck ready.
