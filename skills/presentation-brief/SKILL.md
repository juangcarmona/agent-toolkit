# Presentation brief

Use this skill when the main uncertainty is what the deck should say or how it should flow.

## Output

Produce a short brief with:

- audience and decision or action expected;
- one central message;
- ordered slide purposes;
- evidence, assumptions, and open questions;
- proposed visual form for each slide.

Do not invent facts or polish weak claims into unsupported conclusions. Keep slide titles direct and remove filler, slogans, and presenter narration.

If the user already supplies a deck structure, preserve its requested slide count and order. Hand off to `slidev-deck` (or a PowerPoint deck skill when one is installed) only after the content plan is stable enough to build.
